Install

Connect in under a minute

Same URL everywhere. Pick your assistant.

  1. Open Claude (web or desktop) → Settings → Connectors.
  2. Click Add custom connector, name it Gershon.
  3. Paste the URL above and save. Leave authentication empty.
  4. In a chat, enable the connector from the tools menu and ask your question.

Custom connectors depend on your Claude plan.

  1. Open ChatGPT → Settings → Apps & Connectors → Advanced and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Choose Create, name it Gershon, paste the URL, authentication None.
  3. Start a chat, add the connector from the + menu, and ask.

Developer-mode connectors depend on your ChatGPT plan; menu names can change.

FAQ

What is an MCP server?

The Model Context Protocol is an open standard that lets AI assistants call outside tools. An MCP server is a set of those tools behind one URL. Add the URL to your assistant and it can use them when your question needs them.

Does it cost anything or need an account?

No. It is free, with no signup and no API key.

Where does the data come from?

Public US government sources: USAspending.gov (federal contract spending), Grants.gov (federal funding), BLS QCEW (industry establishments and jobs by state) and SEC EDGAR Form D (private fundraising), plus Gershon Consulting's curated list of US trade shows. LinkedIn tools return search links only.

My company sells to businesses, not to government. Is it useful?

Yes. The US companies winning federal contracts in your category are often the integrators, distributors and resellers a foreign company partners with first, whether or not you ever sell to government.

Can my company apply for US federal grants?

Most federal grants require a US-registered organisation, so foreign companies usually apply through a US subsidiary or a US partner. This is general information, not legal advice.

Can it change anything or read my data?

No. Every tool is read-only. The server only sees the keywords your AI sends and keeps nothing.
