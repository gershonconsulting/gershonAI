Talking Points: walk into every meeting prepared

AI-assisted talking points and messaging guidance for sharper client and prospect conversations.

Olivier Attia, Founder, Gershon ConsultingNovember 18, 20263 min read

Why we built it

All the outreach in the world ends in a meeting, and a meeting is won or lost in the first ten minutes. Preparing well, knowing the company, the person, their likely objections and the angle that will resonate, takes time that busy founders rarely have between calls.

We saw too many hard-won meetings start with "so, tell me about your company". We wanted every one of them to start with something sharper.

What it does

  • Prepares talking points for each meeting from what is known about the company and the person.
  • Suggests the angles, questions and messages most likely to land.
  • Keeps a weekly rhythm so preparation is ready before the calendar fills up.

Why it is powerful

Talking Points turns preparation from an afterthought into a habit. You arrive knowing what matters to the buyer, which makes you more credible and the conversation shorter and more useful for both sides.

In a new market, where you cannot rely on reputation yet, that preparation is often the deciding factor.

Who it is for

Founders, sales leaders and account managers who take many first meetings and want each one to count.
