Pulse: no hot lead waits in a LinkedIn inbox again

LinkedIn and Sales Navigator inbox triage. Every conversation sorted Red, Orange or Green, so the interested prospects get an answer first.

Olivier Attia, Founder, Gershon ConsultingNovember 18, 20263 min read

Why we built it

Good outreach creates a new problem: replies. Hundreds of LinkedIn and Sales Navigator conversations pile up across accounts, and the one prospect who wrote "yes, let's talk" ends up buried between connection thank-yous and polite refusals.

We saw real opportunities go cold simply because nobody answered for three days. In the U.S., three days is long enough for a prospect to take a meeting with someone else.

What it does

  • Reads every LinkedIn and Sales Navigator conversation across your accounts.
  • Sorts each one Red, Orange or Green according to how urgent and promising it is.
  • Puts the interested prospects at the top, ready for a reply or a meeting proposal.

Why it is powerful

Pulse turns an inbox into a to-do list ranked by revenue. Your team stops reading and starts answering. Speed to reply is one of the strongest predictors of a booked meeting, and Pulse makes speed the default.

It also makes outreach measurable: you can see how many conversations are hot, warm or closed, instead of guessing from a scroll.

Who it is for

Anyone running outbound on LinkedIn, especially across several accounts, who cannot afford to miss the reply that matters.
