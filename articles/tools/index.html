Tools: are you getting what you pay for from your growth stack?

One score for every growth tool you pay for: how well each outreach and AI platform is really being used.

Olivier Attia, Founder, Gershon ConsultingNovember 18, 20262 min read

Why we built it

Modern outbound runs on a stack of subscriptions: automation platforms, data providers, email finders, AI models. Each has its own dashboard, its own billing and its own way of quietly failing. We were losing time logging into each one to check that campaigns were really running.

What it does

  • Connects to the outreach and AI platforms you use.
  • Grades how well each one is used, in one dashboard.
  • Shows the raw data behind every grade, plus usage and cost for AI services.
  • Keeps a daily history so trends and breakdowns are easy to spot.

Why it is powerful

Tools makes the invisible visible. A stalled automation or an unused subscription shows up the same day, not at the end of the month. Every dollar in the growth stack is accounted for, and every tool is either earning its place or flagged.

Who it is for

Teams running several outreach and AI tools who want one honest view of what is working.
