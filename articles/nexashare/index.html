NexaShare: make sure the right people see what you publish

LinkedIn content amplification: get your team and network to repost and react to what you publish, at the moment it counts.

Olivier Attia, Founder, Gershon ConsultingNovember 18, 20263 min read

Why we built it

A great post that nobody sees is wasted. On LinkedIn, the first reactions and reposts decide how far a post travels. Most companies have employees and partners happy to help, but no simple way to ask them at the right moment.

What it does

  • Detects new posts from the company pages and people you choose to amplify.
  • Helps your team and network repost and react quickly, in the right way.
  • Reports what was shared, by whom, and what it produced.

Why it is powerful

NexaShare multiplies the reach of every post by the size of your team, without asking anyone to remember anything. Company content stops depending on a handful of enthusiasts and starts working the way LinkedIn rewards: early, genuine engagement from real people.

Paired with Publish and Social, it completes the content cycle: write it, get it seen, measure it.

Who it is for

Companies whose teams and partners want to support their content but need it to take seconds, not effort.
