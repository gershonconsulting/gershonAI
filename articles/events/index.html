Events: stop flying to the wrong U.S. trade shows

From your ideal customer profile to a graded calendar of U.S. events, plus the exhibitors and CEOs worth meeting at each one.

Olivier Attia, Founder, Gershon ConsultingNovember 18, 20263 min read

Why we built it

A U.S. trade show is one of the biggest line items in an expansion budget: booth, flights, hotels, days away from the business. We watched clients pick events by reputation or habit, then come home with a bag of business cards and no meetings with real buyers.

The problem was never effort. It was information. Nobody had checked, before booking, how many of the client's actual buyers would be in the room.

What it does

  • Takes your ICP and scans U.S. trade shows, conferences and industry events.
  • Grades each event on how well its audience matches your buyers, so you can rank them.
  • Pulls the exhibiting companies that fit your profile, with the CEO and decision-maker contacts worth meeting there.

Why it is powerful

Events turns a trade show from a gamble into a plan. You know which shows deserve your budget, and you arrive with a target list instead of hoping the right person walks past your booth. Meetings can be requested weeks before the doors open, which is when the best calendars fill up.

Because it starts from the same ICP as the rest of the Gershon apps, the people you meet at the show flow straight into your LinkedIn outreach and follow-up afterwards. Nothing is lost when the event ends.

Who it is for

Any company that spends money on U.S. events, or is about to, and wants each trip to produce pipeline.
